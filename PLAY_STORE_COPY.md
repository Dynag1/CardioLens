*   View your continuous heart rate with precise, interactive charts.
*   Analyze your resting heart rate (day and night) and **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** to detect recovery trends.
*   Monitor your **Blood Oxygen levels (SpO2)** during sleep and rest.
*   Check your heart rate zones (Rest, Fat Burn, Cardio, Peak).

💤 **Sleep Analysis**
*   Understand your nights with detailed sleep cycle breakdowns (Light, Deep, REM).

🏃 **Activity & Intensity**

🔒 **Total Privacy**

*   No data is sent to external servers or sold to third parties.
*   Direct and secure connection to Fitbit and Google APIs.

✨ **Modern Interface**
*   Clean, intuitive design (Material Design 3).
*   Automatic Dark Mode.
*   Smooth navigation and zoomable charts.

**Why CardioLens?**

**Download CardioLens now and take control of your health!**

**HEALTH CONNECT TRANSPARENCY**
CardioLens requests access to the following Health Connect data to provide its core features:
*   **Heart Rate & HRV**: To display interactive continuous heart rate charts and calculate recovery trends.
*   **Sleep**: To visualize sleep cycles and stages.
*   **Steps & Active Calories**: To provide a daily summary of your physical activity.
*   **Exercise Sessions**: To identify workout periods and analyze heart rate response during effort.

We strictly follow the **Health Connect Limited Use** requirements. Your data is stored **only locally** on your device and is never shared, sold, or uploaded to any server.
